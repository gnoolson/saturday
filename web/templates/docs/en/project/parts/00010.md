<h1 id="main">Project</h1>

A **Project** is the primary unit for organizing resources in **Saturday**. It groups Scripts, Dashboards, Clients, and Schedules into a single logical unit.

Every Script, Dashboard, Client, and Schedule must belong to a specific Project.

A Project can be exported and imported to back up or transfer its contents.
